Programme Deep-Dive
Computer Science
CSC — COMPUTER SCIENCE
B.Sc (Hons) Computer Science
4 Years / 8 Semesters
NUC Accredited · Full-Time
Programme Overview

Computer Science provides a rigorous foundation in computational theory combined with applied technical skills directly relevant to today's industry. Students develop deep expertise in algorithms, programming paradigms, database management, artificial intelligence, and human-computer interaction.

The programme blends foundational research methods with hands-on lab work — ensuring graduates can think both theoretically and apply what they know in real engineering environments.

Graduate Outcomes
Design and implement efficient algorithms and data structures for complex computational problems
Develop intelligent systems leveraging machine learning and artificial intelligence
Design, query, and optimise relational and non-relational database systems
Understand and apply operating system concepts including process management and concurrency

Software Engineering
SEN — SOFTWARE ENGINEERING
B.Sc (Hons) Software Engineering
4 Years / 8 Semesters
NUC Accredited · Full-Time
Programme Overview

Software Engineering is where ideas become products. This programme focuses on the systematic design, development, testing, and deployment of software systems — preparing graduates to build everything from mobile apps to enterprise systems.

Students work in Agile and DevOps environments, collaborate in teams, and tackle real-world software challenges. By graduation, they have shipped real, functional software.

Graduate Outcomes
Design scalable software architectures using industry-standard patterns
Develop full-stack web and mobile applications using modern frameworks
Apply Agile methodologies to manage and deliver software projects
Write comprehensive test suites and implement CI/CD pipelines

Cybersecurity
CYB — CYBERSECURITY
B.Sc (Hons) Cybersecurity
4 Years / 8 Semesters
NUC Accredited · Full-Time
Programme Overview

Cybersecurity is Nigeria's fastest-growing, highest-demand computing discipline. From network security and ethical hacking to digital forensics and cyber law, this programme builds defenders and hunters ready for the real threat landscape.

Students have exclusive access to our dedicated cybersecurity server infrastructure — a hands-on lab where they practise penetration testing, defensive systems, malware analysis, and attack simulations in a safe environment.

Graduate Outcomes
Conduct authorised penetration tests and vulnerability assessments on systems and networks
Design and implement network security architectures and intrusion detection systems
Perform digital forensics investigations on compromised systems
Apply cryptographic principles to secure data in transit and at rest

Entry Requirements
Admission Requirements

Admission into any of the three programmes is processed through the JAMB UTME or Direct Entry routes. Requirements below are as stipulated by NUC and Maduka University's admissions policy.

UTME JAMB Entry Requirements (All Programmes)
→
Candidates must sit for the JAMB UTME and choose Maduka University as first or second choice institution.
→
Required JAMB subjects: Mathematics, English Language, and any two of Physics, Chemistry, Biology, or Economics (varies by programme).
→
Minimum JAMB cut-off mark as stipulated by JAMB and Maduka University for the relevant academic session.
→
Five (5) O'Level credit passes at WAEC/NECO in not more than two sittings, including Mathematics and English.
DIRECT ENTRY Direct Entry Requirements (All Programmes)
→
A'Level results in relevant subjects from NABTEB, Cambridge A-Level, or similar for entry into 200 Level.
→
OND/HND holders in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related fields may also qualify subject to assessment.
→
All Direct Entry candidates must still possess the O'Level requirements (5 credits including Maths and English).
Important Notice
Requirements are subject to annual review by JAMB and Maduka University. Always verify current cut-off marks on the official JAMB and Maduka University admissions portal before applying.

Career Opportunities

A degree from Maduka School of Computing opens doors in Lagos, Abuja, London, and beyond. Here's what your future can look like — and how to get there.

Build intelligent systems for banks, hospitals, agritech. Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch. High-demand in Nigeria and globally.
DB
Database Administrator
Manage and optimise the databases powering Nigerian banks, telcos, and enterprises.
SYS
Systems Analyst
Analyse, design, and improve complex IT systems in government, finance, and telecoms.
RES
Research Scientist / Academic
Pursue postgraduate studies locally or abroad and contribute to global computing research.

Build web and mobile products for startups and enterprises. Most in-demand role in Lagos tech.

Design the systems that scale. Cloud-native, microservices, API-first. Senior roles at top companies.
