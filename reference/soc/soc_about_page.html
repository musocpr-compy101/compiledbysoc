Faculty Overview
Welcome to the School of Computing

The School of Computing at Maduka University is a dynamic and forward-thinking faculty dedicated to producing world-class computing professionals. Established as one of the cornerstone schools of Maduka University, we offer a vibrant academic environment where technology, creativity, and critical thinking converge.

Our programmes are fully accredited by the National Universities Commission (NUC) and are designed to meet the demands of Nigeria's rapidly evolving digital economy. We are committed to equipping every student with the technical skills, ethical grounding, and professional mindset needed to excel in the global technology industry.

Conduct & Compliance
Rules & Regulations

Every student, staff member, and visitor of the School of Computing is expected to abide by these rules at all times. Violations may result in disciplinary action.

Rule 01
Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in all courses to qualify for end-of-semester examinations.
Rule 02
All forms of academic dishonesty — including plagiarism, cheating, and impersonation — are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disciplinary action.
Rule 03
Students must adhere to the approved faculty dress code at all times within school premises. See the Dress Code section for full details.
Rule 04
All laboratory equipment must be handled with care. Any damage caused by negligence will be the responsibility of the student involved.
Rule 05
Mobile phones must be silenced during lectures. Unauthorized use of devices during examinations is a serious offence.
Rule 06
Students are expected to treat all members of the faculty — lecturers, staff, and fellow students — with respect and courtesy at all times.
Rule 07
Departmental dues must be paid in full before students can access certain faculty resources, sit for examinations, or participate in school events.
Rule 08
The use of the school's computer laboratories is restricted to academic purposes only. Unauthorized software installation is prohibited.
