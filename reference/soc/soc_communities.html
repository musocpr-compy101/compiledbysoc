Who We Are
SOC Is a Family

The School of Computing is more than a faculty — it is a community of thinkers, builders, and problem-solvers who share a common belief: that technology, when built with purpose, can change lives. From the first day of 100 Level to graduation day and beyond, every student becomes part of something bigger than their individual degree.

We are a small school with a big heart. Our size is our strength — it means every lecturer knows your name, every student has a voice, and every challenge you face is met by people who genuinely care about your success. The SOC community supports you inside the classroom, outside it, and long after you leave.

Whether you are here to build the next great Nigerian startup, secure the country's digital infrastructure, or push the boundaries of computing research — you belong here. And we are glad you are here.
