# PRO office handover

The platform belongs to the office. Keep an office-owned record of repository, Vercel, Supabase, R2, Paystack merchant account, domain/DNS, email provider, billing and recovery contacts. Use named accounts for staff actions, not shared application logins. Do not put secrets in this document.

The owner appoints executives in `/admin/staff` and grants only their areas. Finance staff manage payment items, reconciliation, records and receipts; academic staff edit academic pages and resources; content staff handle posts/notices/calendar; community staff manage their pages; election officers handle ballot preparation and release. Shared site navigation/settings have a separate grant. Full administrators also manage executive access. Disable departed executives promptly; review provider-level access separately.

All public content is managed inside the website admin. Use the page editor for text/images/links, added blocks for extra content/files, and the publisher for news/journals/notices/rules. Page saves retain revision history. Finance records and notes cannot be overwritten through CMS editing.

Before handover:

1. Verify the successor can sign in and use only their assigned tools. Maintain another trusted administrator for changes to your own access.
2. Transfer billing, recovery, domain renewal, SMTP and merchant-account ownership using each provider's controls.
3. Disable former staff and revoke provider access. Rotate any previously shared credentials.
4. Reconcile the payment register, refunds, disputes and unsettled transactions with Paystack; hand over outstanding enquiries and notes.
5. Transfer content approval, photo consent, student-data retention and election grievance procedures.
6. Rehearse database backup/restore, file visibility and the election emergency stop.
7. Record remaining work: live-provider/browser checks, approved launch content, feedback/RSVP/check-in/polls/advertising workflows and any richer academic catalogue requirements.

Read `FINANCE.md` and `ELECTIONS.md` before operating those features. Do not advertise remaining reference forms as connected services.
